# Billing: SLA service credits

## Eligibility
An enterprise account is owed a credit when an incident that affected its service lasted
more than 4 hours. Confirm the incident's affected services include the account's
workspace service and read the incident's duration.

## Amount
The credit is 10% of the affected month's invoice amount. Never more. Never a full
refund of the month.

## Approval
Credits are consequential: request operator approval for the exact amount.
